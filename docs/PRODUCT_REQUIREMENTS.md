# Product Requirements

The full product specification is [`requirements/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`](../requirements/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
Principle: **create, mix and animate original 3D characters only** — no licensed franchise characters, copied
designs, paid character packs, or assets with unclear commercial-use rights.

Pillars: Create · Mix · Animate · Share. MVP scope and exclusions are listed in plan §1.3; acceptance criteria in
plan §14.5 are tracked in [`IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md).
